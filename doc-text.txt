Please describe the review process as detailed as possible. Our minimum requirements are listed below:
At least two independent reviewers will need to review and approve a paper
A maximum of 4 papers should be assigned to a single reviewer
All the review reports should be submitted to Springer as soon as they will become available (after the notification of acceptance).
 Double-Blind Peer Review Process
All manuscripts submitted to the International Conference on Smart Systems and Sustainable Governance Powered by AI (ICSSG-AI 2027) will undergo a rigorous Double-Blind Peer Review process to ensure the highest standards of academic quality, originality, ethical integrity, and scientific merit. The identities of both authors and reviewers will remain anonymous throughout the review process to guarantee fairness, impartiality, and unbiased evaluation.
1. Initial Editorial Screening
Upon submission, each manuscript will be screened by the Technical Program Committee (TPC) to verify that it:
Falls within the scope of the conference themes.
Complies with the prescribed Springer conference paper formatting guidelines.
Meets submission requirements regarding length, structure, and completeness.
Is anonymized in accordance with the double-blind review policy.
Passes plagiarism and AI writing screening using Turnitin similarity detection software. manuscripts exhibiting unacceptable levels of similarity or ethical concerns will be rejected without entering the peer-review process.
2. Reviewer Assignment
After the initial screening, every eligible manuscript will be sent out to at least two independent experts, with relevant experience in the area of research, to provide a review. The Technical Program Committee and an international panel of subject experts will be used to select reviewers.
To maintain review quality and consistency:
At least two independent reviewers will review each manuscript before editorial decision.
Reviewers will not receive more than four (4) manuscripts for review during the review process, thus allowing reviewers enough time to review manuscripts thoroughly and in good quality. 
Potential conflicts of interest will be thoroughly reviewed before reviewers are assigned. Reviewers that may have personal, professional, institutional or collaborative relationships with the authors will not be assigned to evaluate the related manuscript.
3. Review Criteria
All manuscripts will be assessed by reviewers independently on the following academic and technical criteria (but not limited to):
Originality and novelty of the research.
Technical correctness and scientific quality.
Relevance to the conference themes.
Methodological rigor and soundness.
Innovation and practical significance.
Quality of experimental design, validation, and analysis.
Clarity, organization, and quality of presentation.
Adequacy of references and related work.
Overall contribution to existing knowledge and future research.
Reviewers will make detailed comments and suggestions for improvement and give a recommendation (Accept, Accept with Minor Revision, Accept with Major Revision, or Reject). 
4. Editorial Decision
All reviewers' reports will be considered by the Technical Program Chairs prior to the decision. If the reviewers' recommendations are very different, an extra independent reviewer may be requested or the manuscript may be re-evaluated by the editorial staff to make sure that the decision is fair and balanced. For conditionally accepted papers, authors will be asked to consider all the reviewers' comments and submit a revised manuscript with a letter that clarifies how the comments have been addressed.
Acceptance Criteria
All manuscripts that are accepted after successfully meeting the ICSSG-AI 2027 academic, technical and ethical standards and receive positive comments from the reviewers assigned to them will be scheduled for oral presentation at ICSSG-AI 2027 and will be considered for publication after oral presentation from any one of the authors in the ICSSG-AI 2027 conference proceedings published by Springer Nature upon acceptance, subject to Springer's quality requirements.
6. Submission of Review Reports to Springer
The conference organizing committee will maintain complete documentation of the peer-review process. All reviewer reports, editorial decisions, reviewer assignments, and related review records will be compiled and submitted to Springer immediately after the notification of acceptance and as soon as they become available, in accordance with Springer's Conference Proceedings publication requirements.
7. Review Integrity and Confidentiality
All reviewers will be required to:
Maintain strict confidentiality regarding the manuscripts under review.
Evaluate submissions objectively and solely on their academic merit.
Declare any conflicts of interest prior to accepting review assignments.
Refrain from using unpublished information obtained through the review process for personal or professional advantage.
The ICSSG-AI 2027 Organizing Committee is committed to maintaining a transparent, ethical, and high-quality peer-review system that conforms to internationally accepted scholarly publishing standards and the publication policies of Springer Nature.

Synopsis
Artificial Intelligence is reshaping how software-intensive systems are designed, engineered, and governed. From climate change to cybersecurity, from resource scarcity to digital transformation, AI is being applied at a scale that was simply not possible before. As it embeds itself more deeply in critical infrastructure, industries, and public services, the demand for systems that are trustworthy, ethical, resilient, and sustainable has never been greater.
The book compiles peer-reviewed contributions from researchers, practitioners, industry professionals, and policymakers across computer science, engineering, environmental science, law, and social science. Together, they examine how intelligent systems can be designed, deployed, and governed in ways that are technically sound, environmentally responsible, and genuinely beneficial to society.
The book addresses emerging topics such as machine learning, deep learning, explainable and trustworthy AI, Generative AI, intelligent decision support, big data analytics, AI-enhanced software engineering, cloud-edge computing, cyber-physical systems, digital twins, the Internet of Things, robotics, autonomous systems and smart infrastructure. Application areas include renewable energy, precision agriculture, healthcare, education, transportation, finance, and public administration.
Beyond the technology, the book tackles ethical considerations, algorithmic fairness, privacy, accountability, regulatory compliance, and sustainable governance frameworks 
 making the case that responsible AI governance is as important as the engineering itself.
Distinguishing Features of the Book (in Order of Importance)
Engineering-Centric Approach to AI Systems: This book is very relevant to Artificial Intelligence-enabled Software and Systems Engineering (AI-SSE) and is about the engineering of AI-enabled software-intensive systems, rather than the algorithms or applications themselves.
Coverage of Emerging AI Technologies and Intelligent Systems: The book covers recent developments in generative AI, large language models (LLMs), explainable AI, machine learning, intelligent decision support, Internet of Things, cyber-physical systems, digital twins, cloud-edge systems, autonomous systems, robotics and smart infrastructure 
 all the latest developments in AI research and practice. 
Integration of Technology with Governance and Public Policy: A unique aspect of the book is the seamless integration of engineering innovation, with AI governance, regulatory and legal frameworks, digital governance, risk management, and policy, which helps readers access both the technological and societal aspects of intelligent systems. 
Human-Centric, Trustworthy, and Responsible AI: The book emphasizes explainability, transparency, fairness, accountability, privacy, security, and human oversight, highlighting the technical and societal needs for creating responsible AI-enabled systems. 
Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): The contributions illustrate how AI-powered systems can contribute to sustainable development, by enabling smart and efficient use of resources, renewable energy, resilient infrastructure, environmental monitoring, inclusive digital transformation, and responsible innovation.
Interdisciplinary Research Across Diverse Domains: The book includes contributions from computer science, engineering, environmental science, management, law, public policy, economics and social sciences, thereby encouraging interdisciplinary research and collaboration to solve real-world problems. 
Application-Driven Research with Industrial and Societal Impact: The book provides numerous industrial case studies and examples of domain applications in healthcare, manufacturing, transportation, agriculture, education, finance, commerce, and public administration to show how AI solutions can have measurable impact for industry and society through practical implementations.
A Forward-Looking Resource for Next-Generation Intelligent Systems: 
It brings together a variety of theoretical, engineering, governance and application insights and methods to serve as a comprehensive reference for researchers, software engineers, systems architects, practitioners, educators and policymakers to develop future intelligent, resilient, ethical, and sustainable systems.
